The guides here cover the part of a non-custodial wallet that decides whether you still have the money next year: the backup, the restore, and the checks that tell a real download from a fake one.

Read the backup page before you fund the wallet, not after. A phrase written down and never tested is a hope, and the restore page describes the ten-minute test that turns it into proof.

A Lightning wallet needs two backups rather than one, and the second is the one people forget: the phrase restores keys, and the channel backup restores the balance held in channels. The custody pages explain the difference without assuming any technical background.
